# JSON/YAML recipe Working Group Notes

# Goals of the working group
- Establish best practices for using JYRs
- Identify design opportunities, schema modifications, and potential tooling, to increase the usefulness of JYRs
- Identify and address challenges and missing documentation that prevent wider use of JYRs
- Determine what design choices need to be explicitly stated (and tested for) to keep an acceptable level of compatibility between JSON and YAML recipes.

# History
## 2025-09-11
In the Community Advisory Council meeting a few ideas for augmenting the design
of JSON/YAML recipes were proposed by community member David Crosby, and it was suggested by Jeff Strauss and George Westwater that a working group
be established in the architecture centre as a way to focus the discussion (and
keep a record of decisions and rationale).
